GOT QUESTIONS?

What is Wi-Fi 7? +
Wi-Fi 7 is the latest wireless standard offering speeds up to 46 Gbps, lower latency, and better performance in crowded networks. It's perfect for gaming, 4K/8K streaming, and smart home devices.
How fast is installation? +
Professional installation takes 1-2 hours. We'll schedule a time that works for you, usually within 3-5 business days. Self-installation kits ship within 24 hours and take about 30 minutes to set up.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. If you're on a yearly plan, you'll receive a prorated refund for unused months.
Do you provide the router? +
Yes. All plans include a high-performance Wi-Fi router at no extra cost. START and BOOST plans come with Wi-Fi 6 routers, while ULTRA includes our latest Wi-Fi 7 router.
